# Technical Architecture & Implementation Spec (`SPEC.md`): Red Bull Soapbox Kart Racer

## 1. Executive Summary & Architectural Philosophy
This specification defines a modular, data-driven 3D arcade downhill kart racer built for the browser using [Three.js](https://threejs.org/) (`r187+`), [@dimforge/rapier3d-compat](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@dimforge/rapier3d-compat) (WebAssembly 3D physics), and [Vite](https://vite.dev/). The game marries the chaotic, gravity-assisted downhill obstacles of a Red Bull Soapbox race with tight, arcade *Mario Kart* drift-boost and jump-trick mechanics, structured around a **4-Race Circuit Roguelite Draft Cup**.

### Continual Adaptation & Data-Driven Scaling
The codebase enforces a strict separation between **engine logic** (`/src/core`, `/src/physics`, `/src/controllers`) and **content registries** (`/src/registries`).
- **4-Race Circuit & 3-Way Roguelite Draft Progression (`CircuitManager.js`)**: A full Cup spans a 4-race circuit across the 4 downhill environments. Before each race in the circuit, every human and AI racer enters a **Pre-Race Draft Screen** where they choose 1 of 3 randomly dealt cards:
  1. **Random Permanent Stat Boost** (stacking across all 4 races with calibrated diminishing returns).
  2. **Random Rechargeable Signature Weapon** (such as a **Ballistic Chicken Launcher** or **Baby Oil Puddle Dispenser**) fired on an offensive cooldown throughout the race.
  3. **Random Rechargeable Defensive Shield** (such as a **Bubble-Wrap Bumper**, **Trash-Can Lid Buckler**, or **Tinfoil Faraday Dome**) activated on a defensive cooldown to block/reflect projectiles, negate track hazards, and convert absorbed hits into a slipstream speed surge.
- **Authentic Red Bull Soapbox Mechanics**:
  1. **Pre-Race Push-Start & Judges' Style Score (`StartSequence.js`)**: Rhythm-based 3-2-1 countdown where players mash `Throttle/Trick` to perform a goofy team skit and Pit-Crew Push-Start on the summit ramp, earning a **Judges' Scorecard (`6.0` to `10.0`)** that converts into an instant **Rocket Launch Boost** (or a sputtering smoke stall if over-revved early).
  2. **Piecemeal Cosmetic Shedding & Duct-Tape Repair (`DamageModel.js`)**: Heavy collisions or botched landings dynamically detach non-essential cosmetic child meshes (flying grocery-cart panels, wooden chicken-coop planks, wobbling wheels with cartoon sparks) via temporary physics debris while keeping the kart 100% drivable; hitting a boost pad or activating a shield triggers a **Duct-Tape Snap-Back** animation that welds the parts back onto the chassis.
  3. **Risk-vs-Reward Janky Shortcuts (`ShortcutSpline` branches in `TrackSpline.js`)**: Alternate narrow, elevated soapbox ramps (lodge roofs in Whistler, stair-handrail grinds in San Diego, watchtower battlements on the Great Wall, hippo-back bridges in the Zoo) that shave $3.5\text{–}4.5\text{ s}$ off the run if the entry jump boost is timed, or drop the kart into deep snow/mud off-road penalties if missed.
  4. **Aerodynamic Drafting Slipstream Slingshot (`SlipstreamTracker` in `KartController.js`)**: Tailing within $12.0\text{ m}$ and a $\pm 18^\circ$ rear cone behind any rival kart for $1.5\text{ s}$ builds a visible cartoon wind-tunnel vortex and triggers a $+20\%$ ($1.20\times v_{\text{top}}$) **Slingshot Pass Boost** for $1.8\text{ s}$.
- **Vertical Slice (Milestone 1)**: Implements the complete 4-Race Circuit state machine, 3-Card Pre-Race Draft UI, Judges' Push-Start Sequence, Piecemeal Damage Shedding, Janky Shortcuts, and Slipstream Drafting on **Whistler Ski Run** (plus procedural spline variants for the other 3 stages), 2 soapbox karts (**Rocket Grocery Kart**, **Motorized Chicken Coop**), 2 kid racers, 3 mystery-box pickup items, 4 draftable circuit weapons, 3 draftable defensive shields, 4 draftable stat boosts, and local 1–4 player split-screen against up to 7 AI opponents (8 total racers).
- **Procedural Fallback System (`AssetManager.js`)**: Every kart, detach-piece, character, obstacle, weapon projectile, shield aura, and item registry entry includes a `proceduralFallback(THREE)` builder using primitive Three.js geometries (`BoxGeometry`, `CylinderGeometry`, `ConeGeometry`, `SphereGeometry`, `MeshToonMaterial`). If a `.glb` file in `/public/assets/models/` is not yet generated, the game renders its stylized cartoon primitive mesh immediately so the project is 100% playable at every stage of development.

## 3. Core Mathematical & Physics Specifications

### 3.1 `TrackSpline.js` — 3D Catmull-Rom Downhill Spline, Frenet Extrusion & Janky Shortcuts
The track is a continuous 3D downhill sprint (open-ended point-to-point from Summit $s = 0$ to Finish Line $s = 1$, Monte Carlo verified average completion time $177.0\text{ s}$ across $L = 4,500\text{ m}$).

1. **Cubic Catmull-Rom Formulation**:
   Given control points $\mathbf{P}_0, \mathbf{P}_1, \dots, \mathbf{P}_{N-1} \in \mathbb{R}^3$ with monotonically decreasing overall elevation ($y$-axis) plus local jump ramps, a segment between $\mathbf{P}_i$ and $\mathbf{P}_{i+1}$ for local parameter $t \in [0, 1]$ with tension $\tau = 0.5$ is evaluated via the basis matrix:
   $$\mathbf{C}_i(t) = \frac{1}{2} \begin{bmatrix} 1 & t & t^2 & t^3 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 2 & 0 & 0 \\ -1 & 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 2 & -5 & 4 & -1 \\ -1 & 3 & -3 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{P}_{i-1} \\ \mathbf{P}_i \\ \mathbf{P}_{i+1} \\ \mathbf{P}_{i+2} \end{bmatrix}$$

2. **First & Second Derivatives (Tangent & Curvature)**:
   $$\mathbf{C}'_i(t) = \frac{1}{2} \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 1 & 2t & 3t^2 \end{bmatrix} \mathbf{M}_{\text{CR}} \mathbf{G}_i, \quad \hat{\mathbf{T}}(t) = \frac{\mathbf{C}'_i(t)}{\|\mathbf{C}'_i(t)\|}$$

3. **Parallel-Transport / Banking Frame (Preventing Gimbal Flip)**:
   Compute the horizontal right vector and apply explicit track roll angle $\phi(s)$ (banked turns):
   $$\hat{\mathbf{B}}_0(t) = \frac{\hat{\mathbf{T}}(t) \times \mathbf{u}_{\text{world}}}{\|\hat{\mathbf{T}}(t) \times \mathbf{u}_{\text{world}}\|}, \quad \mathbf{u}_{\text{world}} = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 1 & 0 \end{bmatrix}^T$$
   Rotate $\hat{\mathbf{B}}_0(t)$ around $\hat{\mathbf{T}}(t)$ by banking angle $\phi(s)$ using Rodrigues' rotation formula to obtain the road lateral vector $\hat{\mathbf{B}}(s)$ and road surface normal $\hat{\mathbf{N}}(s) = \hat{\mathbf{B}}(s) \times \hat{\mathbf{T}}(s)$.

4. **Arc-Length Parameterization, Jump Gaps & Janky Shortcut Branches**:
   - Build a lookup table (LUT) of $M = 2000$ samples mapping normalized distance $s \in [0, 1]$ to spline index $(i, t)$ and cumulative distance $d(s) \in [0, L]$.
   - Extrude road cross-section of width $W(s)$ (default $18\text{ m}$) with raised curb colliders ($0.6\text{ m}$ height) and register a static `Rapier.ColliderDesc.trimesh(vertices, indices)` in `PhysicsWorld`.
   - **Jump Gap Segments**: Segments flagged with `isJumpRamp: true` incline sharply ($+18^\circ$ to $+25^\circ$) followed by a lip edge drop-off for airborne trick opportunities.
   - **Risk-vs-Reward Janky Shortcuts (`ShortcutBranches`)**: Each track defines 3 elevated secondary Catmull-Rom sub-splines $\mathbf{C}_{\text{short}, m}(u)$ that bifurcate from main spline parameter $s_{\text{entry}, m}$ and rejoin at $s_{\text{exit}, m}$:
     - Width $W_{\text{short}} = 4.2\text{ m}$ (un-curbed wooden planks, lodge rooftops, or stair rails), arc length $28\%$ shorter than the main path, saving $\approx 3.8\text{ s}$.
     - Entry requires a minimum speed of $0.95 v_{\text{top}}$ or an active Jump Trick / Nitrous Boost; falling off the narrow plank lands the kart on the main road below with a $0.65\times$ deep-snow/mud speed drag for $2.0\text{ s}$.

### 3.2 `RaycastSuspension.js` — 4-Wheel Spring-Damper Physics & Tire Friction
Each kart is represented in [Rapier3D](https://rapier.rs/docs/api/javascript/JavaScript3D/) as a single `RigidBody` (dynamic, base mass $m = 150\text{ kg}$, center of mass offset $\Delta \mathbf{r}_{\text{COM}} = (0, -0.35\text{ m}, 0)$ to prevent rollovers) with a rounded box collider. At each physics step ($\Delta t = 1/60\text{ s}$), 4 downward rays are cast from wheel mount points $\mathbf{w}_k \in \{\text{FL, FR, RL, RR}\}$ in chassis local space:

1. **World-Space Raycast**:
   - Wheel attachment in world space: $\mathbf{p}_k = \mathbf{x}_{\text{chassis}} + \mathbf{R}_{\text{chassis}} \mathbf{w}_k$.
   - Ray direction: $\hat{\mathbf{d}}_{\text{ray}} = -\mathbf{R}_{\text{chassis}} \hat{\mathbf{y}} = -\hat{\mathbf{u}}_{\text{chassis}}$.
   - Max ray length: $L_{\max} = L_{\text{rest}} + R_{\text{wheel}}$ (where $L_{\text{rest}} = 0.45\text{ m}$, $R_{\text{wheel}} = 0.25\text{ m}$).

2. **Hooke's Law Spring + Velocity Damper**:
   If ray $k$ hits the track collider at distance $d_k \le L_{\max}$ with surface normal $\hat{\mathbf{n}}_k$:
   - Suspension compression: $x_k = \max(0, L_{\text{rest}} - (d_k - R_{\text{wheel}}))$.
   - Compression velocity: $v_{\text{comp}, k} = \frac{x_k(t) - x_k(t - \Delta t)}{\Delta t}$ (or projected chassis point velocity $-\mathbf{v}_{\text{wheel}, k} \cdot \hat{\mathbf{n}}_k$).
   - Upward suspension scalar force:
     $$F_{\text{susp}, k} = \max\left(0,\; k_s x_k + c_d v_{\text{comp}, k}\right)$$
     where spring stiffness $k_s = 18,000\text{ N/m}$ and damping coefficient $c_d = 2,400\text{ N}\cdot\text{s/m}$.
   - Apply impulse at point $\mathbf{p}_k$: $\mathbf{J}_{\text{susp}, k} = F_{\text{susp}, k} \, \hat{\mathbf{n}}_k \, \Delta t$.

3. **Lateral Cornering Traction, Drift Slip & Surface Modifiers (Baby Oil Puddles)**:
   - Compute world velocity of wheel contact point: $\mathbf{v}_k = \mathbf{v}_{\text{com}} + \boldsymbol{\omega} \times (\mathbf{p}_k - \mathbf{x}_{\text{com}})$.
   - Project onto wheel lateral right axis $\hat{\mathbf{r}}_k$: $v_{\text{lat}, k} = \mathbf{v}_k \cdot \hat{\mathbf{r}}_k$.
   - Apply lateral counter-impulse:
     $$\mathbf{J}_{\text{lat}, k} = -m_{\text{eff}, k} \, v_{\text{lat}, k} \, \mu_{\text{lat}} \, \hat{\mathbf{r}}_k$$
     where effective per-wheel mass $m_{\text{eff}, k} = 0.25 m$, normal grip $\mu_{\text{lat}} = 0.88 \cdot M_{\text{grip}}$ (scaled by drafted stat boosts), **drifting grip** drops to $\mu_{\text{drift}} = 0.32$ on rear wheels, and **Baby Oil Puddle contact** (unless negated by an active Shield) drops $\mu_{\text{lat}}$ to $0.04$ across all 4 wheels while applying an uncontrollable yaw torque impulse.

### 3.3 `KartController.js`, `StartSequence.js` & `DamageModel.js` — Engine, Drift/Jump FSM, Slipstream Drafting & Soapbox Chaos

1. **Red Bull Soapbox Pre-Race Push-Start & Judges' Style Score (`StartSequence.js`)**:
   - During the $3.5\text{ s}$ pre-race countdown on the summit ramp (`3... 2... 1... DROP!`), a rhythmic gauge oscillates in each player's HUD while 3 cartoon celebrity judges sit at a table beside the ramp.
   - **Style Skit & Pit-Crew Push**: Pressing `Trick/Drift` inside the green rhythm zones triggers a goofy kid dance animation and pit-crew push, raising the **Judges' Scorecard** from $6.0$ up to $10.0$.
   - **Launch Impulse**: Holding `Throttle` inside the final $0.35\text{ s}$ window before `DROP!` triggers a **Soapbox Rocket Start**:
     $$v_{\text{launch}} = \begin{cases} 1.40 \times v_{\text{top}} \text{ for } 1.6\text{ s} & \text{if Score} \ge 9.0 \text{ (Perfect 10! Gold Sparks)} \\ 1.20 \times v_{\text{top}} \text{ for } 1.0\text{ s} & \text{if } 7.5 \le \text{Score} < 9.0 \text{ (Clean Push)} \\ 0.45 \times v_{\text{top}} \text{ for } 0.9\text{ s} & \text{if mashed continuously from } t=3.0\text{ s} \text{ (Blown Gasket Stall!)} \end{cases}$$

2. **Internal Engine Propulsion + Minimal Downhill Gravity Assist + Diminishing-Return Stat Stacking**:
   - Each kart maintains a `CircuitBuildState` containing cumulative stat multipliers drafted across the 4-race circuit:
     $$\mathbf{M}_{\text{stats}} = \{ M_{\text{topSpeed}},\; M_{\text{accel}},\; M_{\text{driftCharge}},\; M_{\text{jumpBoost}},\; M_{\text{knockbackRes}} \}$$
   - **Diminishing-Return Stat Scaling**: The $n$-th drafted stat card ($n \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$) scales by:
     $$\gamma_{\text{dim}}(n) = \max(0.75,\; 1.0 - 0.06 n)$$
   - **Engine & Slope Force**:
     $$F_{\text{engine}} = u_{\text{throttle}} \cdot \left(F_{\text{max}} \cdot M_{\text{accel}}\right) \cdot \left(1 - \left(\frac{\|\mathbf{v}_{\text{forward}}\|}{v_{\text{top}} \cdot M_{\text{topSpeed}}}\right)^2\right)$$
     $$F_{\text{slope}} = m g \sin(-\theta_{\text{pitch}}) \cdot \alpha_{\text{slope}} \quad (\alpha_{\text{slope}} = 0.18,\; \Delta v_{\text{top, slope}} = +3.5\text{ m/s max})$$

3. **Aerodynamic Drafting / Slipstream Slingshot (`SlipstreamTracker`)**:
   - At each step, check all rival karts $j \neq i$.Kart $i$ is inside Kart $j$'s **Slipstream Cone** if:
     $$2.5\text{ m} \le \|\mathbf{x}_j - \mathbf{x}_i\| \le 12.0\text{ m}, \quad \frac{(\mathbf{x}_j - \mathbf{x}_i) \cdot \hat{\mathbf{f}}_i}{\|\mathbf{x}_j - \mathbf{x}_i\|} \ge \cos(18^\circ), \quad \text{and } \|\mathbf{v}_i\| \ge 12.0\text{ m/s}$$
   - While inside the cone, `draftTimer` accumulates at $1.0\text{ s/s}$ (and decays at $0.75\text{ s/s}$ when outside), rendering semi-transparent white wind-ribbon trails streaming off Kart $j$'s rear spoilers onto Kart $i$'s nose.
   - When `draftTimer` reaches $1.5\text{ s}$, trigger a **Slipstream Slingshot Boost**: override top speed to **$1.20 \times v_{\text{top}}$ for $1.8\text{ s}$** with a whooshing air-vortex SFX and reset `draftTimer = 0`.

4. **Mario Kart Style Hop & Drift Boost Finite State Machine (FSM)**:
   - **States**: `NORMAL` $\rightarrow$ `HOPPING` $\rightarrow$ `DRIFTING` $\rightarrow$ `BOOST_RELEASE`.
   - **Transition to `HOPPING`**: Pressing `Drift/Hop (Space / R1 / RB)` while grounded applies an upward impulse $J_{\text{hop}} = m \cdot 4.2\text{ m/s}$. If steering input $|u_{\text{steer}}| > 0.25$ on landing (or during hop), lock drift direction $s_{\text{drift}} = \text{sign}(u_{\text{steer}}) \in \{-1, +1\}$ and enter `DRIFTING`.
   - **Steering Remap in `DRIFTING`**:
     $$\omega_{\text{yaw}} = s_{\text{drift}} \cdot \omega_{\text{base\_drift}} + u_{\text{steer}} \cdot \omega_{\text{modulate}}$$
   - **Charge Accumulation & 3-Tier Mini-Turbo Sparks**:
     $$\frac{dC_{\text{drift}}}{dt} = M_{\text{driftCharge}} \cdot \left(1.0 + 0.65 \cdot \max(0,\; s_{\text{drift}} \cdot u_{\text{steer}})\right)$$
     | Tier | Charge Threshold $C_{\text{drift}}$ | Spark Visual Color | Boost Speed Multiplier | Boost Duration |
     | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
     | **Tier 1 (Mini-Turbo)** | $1.2\text{ s}$ | Electric Blue (`#00d4ff`) | $1.22\times v_{\text{top}}$ | $0.9\text{ s}$ |
     | **Tier 2 (Super Mini-Turbo)** | $2.5\text{ s}$ | Fiery Orange (`#ff7700`) | $1.35\times v_{\text{top}}$ | $1.6\text{ s}$ |
     | **Tier 3 (Ultra Mini-Turbo)** | $4.0\text{ s}$ | Neon Purple/Pink (`#ff00ea`) | $1.50\times v_{\text{top}}$ | $2.4\text{ s}$ |

5. **Jump-Timing Trick Boost (`TrickWindow`)**:
   - Ramp exit zones on `TrackSpline` (and Janky Shortcut entry ramps) are tagged with a trigger volume of length $4.5\text{ m}$ before the ramp lip (`isJumpZone: true`).
   - Pressing `Jump/Trick` within $\Delta t_{\text{trick}} \in [-0.20\text{ s}, +0.12\text{ s}]$ of leaving the lip triggers a 360° kart flip animation and grants a **$1.30 \cdot M_{\text{jumpBoost}} \times v_{\text{top}}$ Trick Boost for $1.2\text{ s}$** upon landing.

6. **Visual "Piecemeal Shedding" Soapbox Damage & Duct-Tape Repair (`DamageModel.js`)**:
   - Every kart mesh defines 4 detachable cosmetic sub-nodes (`Cosmetic_Panel_L`, `Cosmetic_Panel_R`, `Cosmetic_Roof_Spoiler`, `Cosmetic_Bumper_Prop`) and a visual wheel-camber wobble parameter.
   - Whenever the kart suffers a weapon hit, obstacle collision, or hard off-camber landing ($\|\Delta \mathbf{v}_{\text{impact}}\| > 8.5\text{ m/s}$), `DamageModel.detachNextPart()` pops one cosmetic panel off the kart, launching a lightweight visual debris clone spinning through the air while adding a comical $\pm 8^\circ$ visual wobble (`Math.sin(t * 28) * 0.14`) to the wheel meshes and trailing cartoon sparks.
   - **Zero Unfair Speed Penalty + Duct-Tape Snap-Back**: Shedding parts is 100% cosmetic comedy (never permanently crippling speed), and triggering any **Drift Mini-Turbo**, **Nitrous Boost**, or **Shield Activation** plays a silver **Duct-Tape Cross-Strap** visual effect that snaps all detached parts back onto the kart!

### 3.4 `CircuitManager.js` & `ItemManager.js` — 4-Race Circuit Draft (Stat vs. Weapon vs. Shield) & Simulation-Tuned Combat

#### 1. 4-Race Circuit Structure & Scoring
- A Circuit Cup consists of **4 sequential downhill races** (`Race 1` $\rightarrow$ `Race 2` $\rightarrow$ `Race 3` $\rightarrow$ `Race 4`).
- Finishing positions award points per race: **1st**: 15 pts, **2nd**: 12 pts, **3rd**: 10 pts, **4th**: 8 pts, **5th**: 6 pts, **6th**: 4 pts, **7th**: 2 pts, **8th**: 1 pt.
- **Pre-Race 3-Card Draft Phase (`DRAFT_PHASE`)**: Before each of the 4 races, each human player (on their split-screen quadrant) and each AI racer is dealt **3 random cards**:
  - **Card A — Random Permanent Stat Boost**: Stacks across all remaining races in the circuit.
  - **Card B — Random Rechargeable Signature Weapon**: Equips (or upgrades by $+34\%$ effectiveness and $-16\%$ cooldown per extra level) a goofy offensive weapon mounted to the kart.
  - **Card C — Random Rechargeable Defensive Shield**: Equips (or upgrades by $+27\%$ surge/reflection power and $-15\%$ cooldown per extra level) a goofy defensive shield generator.

#### 2. Draftable Circuit Weapons (`CircuitDraftRegistry.js` — Offensive Hardpoint Slot)
Fired via **Fire Weapon** (`E / X / Square`), granting both an immediate recoil/slipstream surge and a **Hit-Confirm Adrenaline Surge** ($+18\%$ speed for $0.8\text{ s}$) upon striking a rival:
1. **Ballistic Chicken Launcher (`ballistic_chicken`)** — *Cooldown: $7.5\text{ s}$ | Range: $65\text{ m}$*:
   - **Mechanic**: Fires a squawking, aerodynamic rubber-beaked chicken forward along the track spline at $52\text{ m/s}$ with homing toward the nearest opponent within $65\text{ m}$ and $\pm 15^\circ$ forward cone.
   - **Impact Effect**: Explodes in a cloud of cartoon feathers, causing a $1.75\text{ s}$ airborne knock-up ($J_y = m \cdot 7.5\text{ m/s}$), shedding a cosmetic kart panel, and blinding their camera with stuck feathers for $2.0\text{ s}$.
   - **Level 2+ Upgrade**: Fires a **3-Chicken Spread Volley** and reduces cooldown by $16\%$ per level.
2. **Baby Oil Puddle Dispenser (`baby_oil_puddle`)** — *Cooldown: $6.5\text{ s}$ | Effective Hazard Trail: $55\text{ m}$*:
   - **Mechanic**: Squirts a glistening, iridescent $3.8\text{ m}$ wide puddle of baby oil onto the track directly behind the kart (persists for $18\text{ s}$) while giving the dropper a forward recoil squirt boost.
   - **Impact Effect**: Any unshielded kart driving over the puddle has its lateral tire friction $\mu_{\text{lat}}$ reduced to $0.04$ for $1.80\text{ s}$ while receiving a spin torque impulse ($\tau_y = \pm 950\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}$), hydroplaning wildly across the road.

#### 4. Draftable Circuit Stat Boosts (`CircuitDraftRegistry.js` — Stacking Passives)

### 3.7 `SplitScreenRenderer.js` — Local 1–4 Player Viewport, $+30\%$ Expanded FOV Chase Camera & 3-Card Draft UI
1. **Single-WebGLRenderer Scissor Architecture**:
   - Uses one `THREE.WebGLRenderer` with `setScissorTest(true)` and an array of $P \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$ `THREE.PerspectiveCamera` instances (one spring-arm chase camera per human racer).
   - **1 Player**: Full screen $(0, 0, W, H)$, aspect $W/H$.
   - **2 Players**: Horizontal split — Top $(0, H/2, W, H/2)$, Bottom $(0, 0, W, H/2)$.
   - **3–4 Players**: $2 \times 2$ Quadrant grid — Top-Left, Top-Right, Bottom-Left, Bottom-Right $(W/2, H/2)$ each (in 3P mode, the 4th quadrant renders a live Track Minimap & Circuit Standings camera).
2. **$+30\%$ Expanded Field of View (FOV) & Pulled-Back Spring-Arm Chase Camera**:
   - Each player's `THREE.PerspectiveCamera` is configured with a **$+30\%$ wider base FOV of $\text{FOV}_{\text{base}} = 91^\circ$** (expanding dynamically up to **$\text{FOV}_{\text{boost}} = 108^\circ$** during Drift Mini-Turbos, Trick Boosts, Slipstream Slingshots, or Nitrous surges via smooth linear interpolation $\text{lerp}(\text{fov}, \text{targetFov}, 8 \Delta t)$).
   - The spring-arm chase offset is pulled back by $30\%$ in chassis local space to $\Delta \mathbf{p}_{\text{cam}} = (0,\; +3.4\text{ m},\; +7.8\text{ m})$ with a forward look-at target $14.0\text{ m}$ down the slope, giving players panoramic peripheral awareness of incoming projectiles, slipstream wind trails, Janky Shortcut ramps, and Baby Oil Puddles across all split-screen quadrants.
3. **Per-Viewport Triple-Slot HUD, Judges' Scorecard & 3-Card Pre-Race Draft Overlay**:
   - **Pre-Race Draft Overlay**: Displays three interactive cartoon cards: **[Card 1: Stat Boost]** vs. **[Card 2: Cooldown Weapon]** vs. **[Card 3: Cooldown Shield]**.
   - **Summit Push-Start HUD**: Displays the rhythmic push bar and animated **Celebrity Judges' Scorecards (`9.5 / 10!`)** during the 3-2-1 countdown.
   - **Race HUD**: Displays Current Race Rank (`1st / 8`), Circuit Stage (`Race 2 of 4`), Downhill Progress Bar (`0% -> 100%`), Speedometer, Slipstream Draft Meter, **Slot 1: Mystery Box Pickup**, **Slot 2: Cooldown Circuit Weapon**, **Slot 3: Cooldown Circuit Shield**, Active Stat Boost badges, and Drift Boost Charge Gauge.
4. **Multi-Input Mapping (`InputManager.js`)**:
   - Polls `navigator.getGamepads()` for up to 4 standard gamepads:
     - Left Stick: Steer | `R2/A`: Throttle | `L2`: Brake | `R1/RB`: Hop/Drift/Trick/Push-Start | `L1/LB`: Use Mystery Item | `X/Square`: Fire Circuit Weapon | `B/Circle/Y`: Activate Circuit Shield.
   - Keyboard fallback supports 2 simultaneous local keyboard players:
     - **Player 1**: `WASD` Steer/Throttle, `Space` Hop/Drift/Trick/Push-Start, `Left Shift` Use Mystery Item, `E` Fire Circuit Weapon, `Q` Activate Circuit Shield.
     - **Player 2**: `Arrow Keys` Steer/Throttle, `Enter` Hop/Drift/Trick/Push-Start, `Right Shift` Use Mystery Item, `/` Fire Circuit Weapon, `.` Activate Circuit Shield.
